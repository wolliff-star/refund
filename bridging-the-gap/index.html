Executive Summary

83% Experienced delays

Government funding is a critical source of support for nonprofit organizations and Tribes delivering essential publicly funded programs.

Respondent Profile

  • 67 percent are Indigenous-led
  • 59 percent are BIPOC-led
  • 28 percent are woman-led
  • 10 percent are LGBTQIA-led

82 percent meet at least one marginalized leadership definition.

Financial Strain

  • 74 percent drew on reserves
  • 40 percent took out loans
  • 20 percent used personal finances

Operational Impact

  • 49 percent cut programs
  • 26 percent delayed payments
  • 23 percent reduced staff
  • 17 percent laid off staff
